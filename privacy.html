Privacy Policy – Video Tool Pro Max

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Video Tool Pro Max ("the tool") is a desktop application used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data the tool accesses

When you sign in with Google, the tool requests access to your YouTube account through the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, set thumbnails and add videos to playlists on your channel;
  • read your channel's videos, upload schedule and public statistics;
  • read your channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers).

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload/schedule videos and to create reports for the channel owner.
  • Sign-in tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the tool.
  • The tool does not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties and does not use it for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access / deleting data

You can revoke the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's folder on your computer removes all stored tokens and reports.
